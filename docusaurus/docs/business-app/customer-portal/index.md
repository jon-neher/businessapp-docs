# Customer Portal

The Customer Portal is a centralized hub where you can manage all of your client interactions in one place. Built by Vendasta, this feature gives you everything you need to stay on top of customer relationships.

## Getting Started

To access the Customer Portal, navigate to your Partner Center dashboard and select **Customer Portal** from the left sidebar. If you don't see it, contact your reseller to have the feature enabled for your account.

Previously, managing customer interactions required switching between multiple tools. The Customer Portal consolidates everything into a single view so you can work more efficiently.

## Features

### Client Directory

The Client Directory displays all of your active clients. You can search, filter, and sort by name, status, or last interaction date.

1. Open the Customer Portal from the sidebar.
2. Select **Client Directory** at the top of the page.
3. Use the search bar to find a specific client.

### Interaction Timeline

Every client profile includes a timeline that shows all past interactions — emails, calls, form submissions, and notes. This used to be spread across different sections, but now it's unified.

### Quick Actions

From any client profile, you can:

- Send an email directly from the portal
- Schedule a follow-up call
- Add an internal note
- Assign the client to a team member

## Permissions

Access to the Customer Portal is controlled by your Vendasta account settings. Administrators can grant or restrict access for individual team members through the Partner Center.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I export client data?**
A: Yes. Go to **Client Directory** > **Export** and choose your preferred format. Formerly, exports were limited to CSV only, but PDF and Excel are now supported as well.

**Q: Who can see interaction history?**
A: Any team member with portal access can view the timeline. Your reseller may have additional visibility controls configured.
