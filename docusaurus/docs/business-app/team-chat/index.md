# Team Chat

Team Chat lets you communicate with your team members in real time -- right inside Business App. Send messages, share files, and keep everyone on the same page without switching to a separate messaging tool.

## Getting Started

To open Team Chat, select the chat icon in the top navigation bar. If this is your first time using Team Chat, you will be prompted to set up your profile -- including a display name and notification preferences.

Previously, team communication required an external tool like Slack or email. Team Chat brings conversations directly into your workspace.

## Channels

Channels organize conversations by topic. You can create channels for specific projects, departments, or anything else that makes sense for your team.

### Creating a Channel

1. Open Team Chat from the navigation bar.
2. Click the + icon next to Channels.
3. Enter a channel name and optional description.
4. Choose whether the channel is public or private.
5. Click Create.

### Channel Settings

Each channel has settings that control notifications, member access, and pinned messages. To access these settings, click the gear icon at the top of the channel view.

## Direct Messages

You can also send direct messages to individual team members or create group conversations with up to 10 people. Direct messages are private and only visible to the participants.

## Sharing Files

Drag and drop files directly into any conversation to share them with your team. Team Chat supports images, PDFs, spreadsheets, and most common file types. The maximum file size is 25 MB.

## Notifications

Team Chat sends notifications for new messages in channels you've joined and all direct messages. You can customize notification settings per channel — for example, muting a busy channel while keeping alerts on for direct messages.

To adjust notifications:

1. Open the channel or conversation.
2. Click the bell icon.
3. Select your preferred notification level.

## Integration with Vendasta Products

Team Chat integrates with other tools in your dashboard, so you can share reports, customer profiles, and campaign results directly in a conversation without leaving the app.

## Tips

- Pin important messages in a channel so they're easy to find later
- Use @mentions to notify specific team members
- Star conversations you want to quick-access from the sidebar
